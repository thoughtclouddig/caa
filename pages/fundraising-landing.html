What's Next

Whatever you do in aviation, the schedule rarely cooperates with Sunday.

A pilot, a mechanic, a controller, a flight attendant, a missionary aviator: the shift changes, but the isolation doesn't. Right now, there's still no easy way for a Catholic in any of those roles to find the others and say, "here is where I belong." You've probably felt that gap yourself, and you've probably also felt the opposite: showing up somewhere and finding out another Catholic in aviation was already there, already praying, already building the same thing you were trying to build alone.

That's what a chapter does. That's what a scholarship does for a kid who couldn't otherwise afford flight training. CAA exists to witness to the Good News of Jesus Christ in the world of aviation, and that's what it's always meant in practice, going back to the bylaws we filed in 2012. The Church is universal. This community should be too.

What we're building now is the platform that makes that easier to find, in whatever city you land in, and the funding to keep the chapters and clubs and scholarships going while we build it.

What Your Gift Funds

Where the money actually goes.

Platform & brand build: 40% Chapter & CAC starter support: 20% Scholarship fund seed: 20% First Aviation Mass: 10% Content & launch ops: 10%

Platform & brand build ($8,000)

The website itself, finished and live: the Mass finder, the member map, the chapter directory, mentorship tools. The thing that makes CAA findable.

Chapter & CAC support ($4,000)

Starter kits for new chapters, plus seed money for local Cupertino Aviation Clubs, the flying clubs where members actually train and fly at low cost.

Scholarship fund ($4,000)

The first deposit into CAA's scholarship fund, so a kid who couldn't otherwise afford flight training gets a real shot at it.

First Aviation Mass ($2,000)

One Mass, celebrated by chapters everywhere on the same weekend. Small cost, but it's the first time this happens worldwide.

Content & launch ($2,000)

Moving existing members and content over, and getting the first stories and formation resources ready before launch day.

The Mission Hasn't Changed

CAA was built on Faith, Flying and Fellowship, going back to the bylaws we filed in 2012: low-cost flight training through local flying clubs, mentoring young people, and a scholarship fund for the next generation of Catholic pilots. This campaign pays for the platform on top of that, plus real money into the chapters, clubs, and scholarships that were the point all along.

The website build ($8,000 of this) moves fast once it's funded. The rest, chapters, clubs, the scholarship fund, the first Aviation Mass, keeps going after that, for as long as the giving does.
